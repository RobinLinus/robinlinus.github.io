Some stuff I do

  • BitVM: Compute Anything on Bitcoin.
  • BitVM Bridge: Bridging Bitcoin to Second Layers. A collaboration with Matteo Maffei and his Security and Privacy group at TU Wien (presented at the Science of Blockchain Conference at Berkeley). This work received the Bitcoin Research Prize 2025.
  • BitVM3: Efficient computation on Bitcoin using garbled circuits.
  • BitVM-448: Covenant-Based BitVM3 Bridges
  • ZeroSync: A STARK proof to verify Bitcoin's chain state in an instant.
  • Stakechains: A Bitcoin-backed proof-of-stake algorithm based on one-time Schnorr signatures (presented at CoDecFin'22). Also co-authored a research paper on this topic with David Tse and his group at Stanford University.
  • zkCoins: A payment system with strong privacy and scalability, combining a client-side validation protocol with validity proofs. Co-authored a research paper on this topic with Liam Eagen and Jonas Nick from Blockstream Research.
  • BitStream: Decentralized file hosting incentivised via Bitcoin payments.
  • VRF in the Scalar: A protocol for fair data exchange using a verifiable random function in the scalar.
  • Binohash: Transaction introspection without softforks. Using Bitcoin's variable-length ECDSA DER encoding as a side channel to read transactions in Bitcoin script.
  • I am a Ph.D. candidate at Stanford University, focusing on the scalability, privacy, and usability of Bitcoin.

Other stuff I did for fun

Bitcoin

  • Designing protocols on top of Bitcoin such as improved stealth addresses, light clients, or an OR operator for DLCs and PTLCs based on verifiable encryption.
  • Some Day Peg: Betting on the activation of a two-way peg bridging BTC to other chains. Co-created with Burak and Super Testnet.
  • Bitcoin DEX (backend is down). A trading protocol based on non-interactive atomic swaps using sighash_single. A Bitcoin SPV client in Solidity for a trustless two-way peg between Ethereum and Bitcoin.
  • Simple Bitcoin web app. Usability research on key management, using secure key generation from email and password via a WarpWallet, and storing credentials in the Browser's password manager. A related work is Secure Bookmarks, co-created with Egor Homakov.
  • Exotic Bitcoin Scripts such as TicTacToe in a Schnorr signature, or nondeterministic programming.
  • OP_CAT magic such as emulating covenants using ECDSA, emulating EC multiplication, and exploring Evil Covenants.
  • Dark Skippy: A novel mechanism for key exfiltration attacks. Co-created with Lloyd Fournier and Nick Farrow.
  • Mechanisms for replay protection based on dust inputs, or reserved nLocktime ( implemented by ecash.com)
